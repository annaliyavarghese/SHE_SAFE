Your everyday safety checklist

Four steps to feel more prepared

Create a safety plan that suits your circumstances and update it whenever you need to.

01

Choose your people

Pick two or three trusted people and agree on how you will get in touch.

02

Plan your journey

Check your route, transport options and a backup plan before leaving.

03

Protect your privacy

Use a strong device passcode and review location-sharing settings.

04

Know where to turn

Save verified local emergency numbers and support services.
